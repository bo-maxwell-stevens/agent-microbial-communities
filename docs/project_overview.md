These are primers and lengths used in the sequencing:
AMF: WANDA/AML2
16S (2x250): 515F (GTGYCAGCMGCCGCGGTAA)-926R (CCGYCAATTYMTTTRAGTTT)
General 18S (2x250): Euk575F (ASCYGYGGTAAYWCCAGC) ja Euk895R (TCHNHGNATTTCACCNCT)
ITS (PacBio, amplicon length ~780bp): ITS9mun (TGTACACACCGCCCGTCG)-ITS4ngsUni (TCCTSCGCTTATTGATATGC)
trnL (2x150): trnLg (GGGCAATCCTGAGCCAA) - trnLh (CCATTGAGTCTCTGCACCTATC)

There is a study metadata table (Final_data_with_diversity_prefixed.csv), with environmental data:
Plant diversity metrics (alpha, gamma, dark, compl, pool, beta)
Soil chemistry (N_pct, C_pct, pH_KCL, P_Mehlich3_mg_100g, K_Mehlich3_mg_100g)
Human footprint metric (hfp.300)
Environmental data (bio1now.100: temperature, bio12now.100: precipitation)
Location (lat, long)
