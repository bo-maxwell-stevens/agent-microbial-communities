## Figure 1
- **Source:** `results/phase5d_synthesis/final_coupling_network.png` (1600x1200)
- **Scientific purpose:** Integrated cross-domain coupling strengths across pairings identify BAC↔ITS and EUK↔ITS as strongest coupled combinations.
- **Likely weaknesses:** potential color-scale saturation, uncertainty intervals not explicit in all panels, and limited branch-contrast annotation.
- **Publication-quality improvements:** standardize typography and panel labels; use colorblind-safe palette; add explicit uncertainty legends and sample-size annotation; ensure vector-friendly export workflow for journal production.
