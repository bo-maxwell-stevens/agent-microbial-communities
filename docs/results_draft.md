# Results Draft

## Results 1 — Cross-domain coupling
- Cross-domain coupling metrics were non-uniform across pair-branch combinations (Figure 1; Table 1).
- The highest combined coupling strength was observed for BAC↔ITS (presence/absence) at 0.574.
- The second and third highest coupling strengths were EUK↔ITS (presence/absence, 0.538) and EUK↔ITS (CLR, 0.535).
- The strongest Mantel relationship was BAC↔ITS (presence/absence) with Mantel = 0.584 and p = 0.002.
- The strongest Procrustes similarity was EUK↔ITS (CLR) with similarity = 0.683.
- Across threshold shifts (0.05 to 0.10), absolute Mantel changes were small (median 0.044, maximum 0.084), and absolute Procrustes-fit changes were also small (median 0.021, maximum 0.041).
- For BAC↔ITS, Mantel was 0.584 (presence/absence) and 0.358 (CLR), with the presence/absence branch ranking highest overall.
- For AMF↔ITS, Mantel was 0.459 (presence/absence) and 0.525 (CLR).

## Results 2 — BAC integration
- BAC integration changed pair ordering relative to AMF-centered-only interpretation by introducing BAC-inclusive combinations at the top of the coupling hierarchy (Figure 1; Table 1).
- BAC-inclusive pairings occupied 2 of the top 4 coupling-strength positions.
- BAC↔ITS (presence/absence) combined Mantel 0.584 and Procrustes similarity 0.564, yielding coupling strength 0.574.
- Compared with AMF↔ITS (presence/absence), BAC↔ITS (presence/absence) showed higher Mantel (0.584 vs 0.459) but lower Procrustes similarity (0.564 vs 0.430).

## Results 3 — Environmental drivers
- Environmental driver summaries were derived from dbRDA model outputs and pair-level rankings (Figure 2; Table 3; Table 4).
- The highest dbRDA adjusted R² was 0.278 for BAC↔ITS (CLR), while the lowest was 0.188 for AMF↔EUK (CLR).
- pH contribution ranged from 0.092 to 0.151 across pair-branch combinations.
- In pair-level rankings, the top geography-sensitivity mean adjusted R² was 0.271, and all pair-level permutation p-values were 0.002.
- The largest mean predictor delta adjusted R² was for pH_KCl (0.111), followed by lat (0.013) and lon (0.012).

## Results 4 — Plant-diversity hypotheses
- Plant-diversity comparisons across hypotheses A–G used completed phase5c and phase5d summaries only (Figure 3; Figure 4; Figure 5; Table 5).
- Among hypotheses A–G, hypothesis B ranked first with mean delta adjusted R² = 0.013, and hypothesis F ranked second with 0.011.
- Across pair-branch summaries, mean effect sizes followed alpha (0.013) > pool (0.009) > dark (0.003) > compl (-0.004).
- The strongest pair-specific plant-diversity increment was AMF↔ITS (presence/absence) with delta adjusted R² vs abiotic base = 0.019.

## Results 5 — Integrated synthesis
- Integrated synthesis combined coupling, environmental, and plant-diversity hierarchies from final phase5d outputs (Figure 1; Figure 2; Figure 3; Table 6).
- The highest integrated coupling hierarchy value in the final synthesis was 0.574 for BAC↔ITS (presence/absence).
- The lowest integrated coupling hierarchy value was 0.400 for AMF↔EUK (presence/absence).
- Environmental hierarchy values in final synthesis ranged from 0.188 to 0.278, while plant-diversity added variation ranged from 0.007 to 0.019.
