# Discussion Outline (Structure Only)

## 1) Main biological findings
- Cross-domain coupling is heterogeneous, not uniform across pairs/branches.
- BAC↔ITS and EUK↔ITS occupy top coupling tiers under current synthesis metrics.
- AMF-centered pairs are generally weaker in coupling magnitude but retain plant-associated signal.
- Environmental and plant layers explain different parts of the observed structure.

## 2) BAC↔ITS interpretation
- Present BAC↔ITS as strongly coupled and strongly environment-structured.
- Evaluate biological-interaction interpretation as plausible but not uniquely supported.
- Emphasize that high BAC↔ITS coupling is compatible with shared abiotic filtering.
- Tie interpretation directly to Phase 5B adjusted R² and Phase 5D synthesis outputs.

## 3) AMF-centered interpretation
- Frame AMF-centered patterns as ecologically distinct, not absent.
- Highlight lower coupling magnitude relative to BAC↔ITS/EUK↔ITS.
- Retain plant-association signal as supportive but modest evidence.
- Avoid overgeneralizing AMF-specific conclusions beyond observed effect sizes.

## 4) Environmental filtering interpretation
- pH-linked signal as recurrent integrative axis across pair/branch contexts.
- Environmental adjusted R² provides explanatory structure for coupling differences.
- Clarify geography-sensitivity gains as contextual explanatory improvements.
- Explicitly separate environmental structure from direct biotic-interaction claims.

## 5) Plant-diversity interpretation
- Plant-diversity terms provide incremental, non-dominant explanatory gain.
- Alpha-associated models rank highest in completed comparisons.
- Keep interpretation as context-dependent contribution beyond abiotic base.
- Discuss potential predictor non-independence and avoid exclusivity language.

## 6) Dark-diversity implications
- Dark/pool/completeness metrics broaden interpretation beyond observed richness.
- Dark-diversity effects appear weaker than alpha/pool in current summaries.
- Interpret dark-diversity signals as hypothesis-generating ecological context.
- Connect to assembly-limitation framing without claiming direct mechanism.

## 7) Caveats and limitations
- Correlative, synthesis-level framework (no manipulative confirmation).
- Coupling composite metric can mask Mantel-Procrustes divergence if overused.
- Compositional and sparsity constraints may affect distance/coupling behavior.
- Modest ΔR² values require conservative biological significance framing.
- AMF marker/taxonomic resolution constraints may limit domain-specific inference.
- Rule-based synthesis labels are communicative, not inferential tests.

## 8) Future directions
- Targeted validation experiments for BAC↔ITS interaction vs shared filtering mechanisms.
- Longitudinal or perturbation designs to test temporal/causal directionality.
- Additional robustness frameworks for metric discordance and predictor redundancy.
- AMF-focused resolution improvements and trait-linked interpretation.
- Mechanistic integration of environmental gradients with plant-diversity assembly processes.
