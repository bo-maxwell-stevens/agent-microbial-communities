# Final figure captions (V5)

## Figure 1. Integrated cross-domain coupling hierarchy across domain-pair and branch combinations.
This figure shows ranked integrated coupling values for BAC↔ITS, EUK↔ITS, AMF↔ITS, and AMF↔EUK under presence/absence and CLR branches, derived from completed synthesis outputs. It demonstrates relative ordering of association-level concordance in this cohort. It does **not** demonstrate causal interaction mechanisms.

## Figure 2. Environmental model layer for pair-branch combinations.
This heatmap summarizes completed dbRDA-derived environmental structure metrics (including adjusted R² and top-ranked predictors) for each pair-branch combination. It shows that environmental covariates explain substantial variation in coupling-relevant structure. It does **not** partition direct biotic interaction from shared abiotic filtering.

## Figure 3. Plant-diversity hypothesis comparison (A–G) as incremental explanatory gain.
This figure reports Δ adjusted R² relative to abiotic baselines across pair-branch contexts. It shows magnitude differences among candidate plant-context formulations, with alpha-inclusive models often ranking higher in this dataset. It does **not** establish that any plant metric is a primary causal driver.

## Figure 4. Distribution of plant-context Δ adjusted R² by domain pairing.
This panel shows the scale and consistency of plant-associated increments across BAC↔ITS, EUK↔ITS, AMF↔ITS, and AMF↔EUK contexts. It demonstrates that effects are generally modest and pair-dependent. It does **not** imply uniform biological effect strength across ecosystems.

## Figure 5. Coupling magnitude versus plant-responsiveness plane.
This joint view contrasts integrated coupling magnitude with plant-associated incremental signal for each pairing. It shows that AMF-linked pairings can have lower total coupling but relatively higher plant-responsiveness increments. It does **not** provide mechanistic proof of AMF-mediated causation.

## Supplementary Figure S1. Provenance map of completed analysis phases.
This diagram shows the linkage among completed Phase 2/4 coupling outputs, Phase 5B environmental summaries, Phase 5C plant-diversity outputs, and Phase 5D integration. It documents traceability and workflow provenance only.

## Supplementary Figure S2. Stability of hypothesis ranking across pair-branch slices.
This supplementary panel shows how hypothesis rankings vary across pair-branch subsets. It demonstrates robustness limits and context dependence of plant-diversity ranking behavior. It does **not** establish a universally dominant hypothesis class.
