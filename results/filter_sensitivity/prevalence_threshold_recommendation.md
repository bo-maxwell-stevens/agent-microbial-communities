# Prevalence Threshold Recommendation

## Canonical threshold
- Canonical primary threshold: **prevalence ≥ 0.05**.

## Sensitivity analyses retained
- **No filtering** and **prevalence ≥ 0.10** are retained as sensitivity analyses.

## Cross-threshold stability
- Major conclusions were stable across all thresholds.
- pH_KCl remained the top environmental predictor.
- abiotic_plus_alpha remained the top plant-diversity model.
- AMF↔ITS remained the most plant-responsive pair.
- No Phase 5B/5C significance calls were gained or lost.

## Justification
- prevalence ≥ 0.05 reduces extreme rare-taxon noise while retaining more community information than prevalence ≥ 0.10.
