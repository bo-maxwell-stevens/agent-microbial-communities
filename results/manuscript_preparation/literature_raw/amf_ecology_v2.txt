Abstract: 
 Mycorrhiza is a mutualistic symbiosis between some plant species and a diverse group of soil fungi. Mycorrhizal symbiosis is categorized into discrete types based on the structural differences and the taxonomic position of involved plants and fungi. The main types of mycorrhiza include arbuscular, ericoid, orchid, monotropoid and ectomycorrhiza, the latter being the main focus of this review. Identifying ectomycorrhizal fungi (EMF) is crucial for understanding the ecology and preservation of rare and endangered plants, fungi and habitats.
